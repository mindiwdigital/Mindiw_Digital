Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about website projects, packages and delivery.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start from LKR 7,500. The final price depends on website type, pages, features, content and project requirements.

How do I start a website project?

Contact us through WhatsApp and explain your idea. We will discuss your requirements, suitable package and next steps.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Our designs are planned to be responsive and adapt to common mobile, tablet and desktop screen sizes.

Do I need to purchase a domain?

A custom domain is usually registered separately. Registration and renewal costs depend on the selected provider and domain extension.

What about hosting and server costs?

Hosting or server services may have separate fees. Requirements and renewal costs will be discussed before the project begins.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Website updates and support can be discussed according to the agreed project and support arrangement. Some changes may involve additional costs.

Can you create an online store?

Yes. Online store projects can be discussed based on product quantity, payment methods, order management and required features.

How long does it take to complete a website?

Completion time depends on project size, required features and how quickly content and feedback are provided.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You can explain your idea in simple language. We will guide you through the information needed.

How can I get a quotation?

Send us your website requirements through WhatsApp or the contact form to discuss a suitable quotation.
